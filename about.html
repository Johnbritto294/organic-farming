Est. 1989 · Willow Valley

Four Generations of Patient Farming

Stackly started as thirty acres and a hand-me-down tractor. Today it's ninety acres of heritage grain, market vegetables, and pastured hens.

Our Philosophy

"Leave the soil richer than you found it."

Great-Aunt Nell set that rule in 1989. Over thirty-five years later, we still farm without synthetic fertilizers, chemical weedkillers, or heavy compaction tillage. We believe healthy, living soil creates nutritious produce that tastes like real food should.

4 Core Pillars

Our Regenerative Soil Ethos

We treat soil as a living organism. Here are the four principles behind every harvest.

PILLAR #1 • SUB-SURFACE TILTH

Zero Tillage Stewardship

By eliminating mechanical tilling, we protect miles of underground mycorrhizal fungal hyphae networks that transfer minerals directly to crop roots.

PILLAR #2 • NITROGEN FIXATION

Year-Round Cover Crops

Crimson clover, hairy vetch, and winter rye cover 100% of our soil during off-seasons, preventing erosion and drawing down atmospheric carbon.

PILLAR #3 • BIODIVERSITY BUFFERS

Wild Pollinator Corridors

Every field boundary features wild lavender, sunflower, and clover borders — providing natural habitat for 12 honeybee hives and native solitary bees.

PILLAR #4 • NATURAL FERTILITY

Rotational Pasture Hen Grazing

Mobile coops rotate 600 pastured hens across clover paddocks every 48 hours, naturally fertilizing the soil while controlling crop pests.
