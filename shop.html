From Soil to Doorstep

12-Hour Freshness Journey

We never store produce in warehouses. Here is how your harvest box moves on delivery day.

06:00 AM

Morning Field Picking

Greens, tomatoes, and microgreens harvested while early dew protects natural sugars.

08:30 AM

Spring Water Washing

Crops washed gently in cold mountain spring water & packed into recyclable straw boxes.

11:00 AM

Van Temperature Control

Loaded directly into eco-chilled delivery vans maintaining 45°F ideal humidity.

02:00 PM

Doorstep Arrival

Delivered to your porch with a handwritten note detailing the morning field routine.
